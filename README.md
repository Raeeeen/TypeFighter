# TypeFighter

TypeFighter is a **typing game where you fight bosses by typing text as fast as possible**.

Each boss has text that the player needs to type. The faster you type and defeat the boss, the faster your completion time will be.

The game includes **Solo** and **Multiplayer** modes, with leaderboards that track your progress and fastest times.

## Solo Mode

In Solo Mode, you fight through **10 floors**, with a different boss on each floor.

Each floor becomes harder because the player's HP is reduced by **10 HP per floor**.

| Floor    |     HP | Mistakes Allowed |
| -------- | -----: | ---------------: |
| Floor 1  | 100 HP |      10 mistakes |
| Floor 2  |  90 HP |       9 mistakes |
| Floor 3  |  80 HP |       8 mistakes |
| Floor 4  |  70 HP |       7 mistakes |
| Floor 5  |  60 HP |       6 mistakes |
| Floor 6  |  50 HP |       5 mistakes |
| Floor 7  |  40 HP |       4 mistakes |
| Floor 8  |  30 HP |       3 mistakes |
| Floor 9  |  20 HP |       2 mistakes |
| Floor 10 |  10 HP |        1 mistake |

In TypeFighter, **HP represents how many times you can make a mistake while typing**.

For example, on Floor 10 you only have **10 HP**, meaning you can only make **one mistake** before losing.

The goal is to reach higher floors while also defeating each boss as quickly as possible.

## Leaderboards

The leaderboard tracks your **highest floor reached** and your **fastest time**.

The main goal is to:

* Reach the highest floor possible.
* Defeat bosses as quickly as possible.
* Improve your typing speed and reduce mistakes.

Your completion time represents **how quickly you defeated the boss**.

## Multiplayer

TypeFighter also includes multiplayer modes where players compete against each other to see who can defeat the boss the fastest.

### Matchmaking

Players can enter multiplayer matchmaking and compete against another player.

Both players fight the same boss and type their text as quickly as possible.

The player who **defeats the boss first wins**.

### Custom Rooms

Players can also create a **custom room** and invite other players using a room code.

Everyone in the room fights the boss using the same basic mechanics.

The difference is that a custom room can have multiple players competing at the same time.

If a player defeats the boss first, they can **sit back and watch the remaining players continue their fights**.

Once everyone finishes, the game displays the players **ranked by their completion time**, showing who defeated the boss the fastest.

## Game Modes

### Solo

Fight through 10 floors with different bosses and increasingly limited HP.

### Multiplayer Matchmaking

Compete against another player to see who can defeat the boss first.

### Custom Room

Create a room, invite players using a room code, and compete with everyone in the room.

## Project Purpose

TypeFighter was created as a typing game focused on **speed, accuracy, and competition**.

The main idea is simple: **type faster, make fewer mistakes, and defeat the boss before your opponent.**

The game combines typing mechanics with boss fights and competitive leaderboards to make typing feel more like a game rather than a traditional typing test.
